# ThinkMedia

## Users
Students prepare for OJT, companies review their own applicants, instructors browse resources and administrators verify and moderate.

## Product
A career workspace joining learning, discovery, applications and guidance. SRS cumulative PD7, tables 89–97.

## Constraints
React + FastAPI + PostgreSQL. Preserve DeepSeek backend. No deployment, external orchestrators, real messaging or production mutations. 360px and desktop, English and Asia/Manila dates.

## Ethical stance
Private applications stay out of AI prompts. No automated hiring decisions, fabricated evidence or self-assigned administration. Clearly distinguish unavailable features from live workflows.

## Success
Verified company publishes; student applies once; owning company records a valid decision; access and transaction checks pass.

## Open questions
Which retention policy and verification evidence will govern the pilot? Which LLM provider and budget are approved? Who will consent to evaluation data collection?
